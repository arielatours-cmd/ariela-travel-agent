Terms of Use

Working draft for legal review. These terms explain Ariella’s service and the division of responsibility between Ariella, users and travel providers.

1. Service

Ariella discovers, compares, filters and monitors flight offers and vacation-planning information, using third-party data and automated or AI-based tools where appropriate.

Ariella is not an airline, does not operate flights and does not issue flight tickets. Unless expressly stated otherwise, flight purchases are completed with an airline, online travel agency or other third-party provider.

2. Prices and availability

Prices, availability, schedules, routes, connections, baggage, taxes, fees and change/cancellation conditions come from external sources and may change at any time. Ariella aims to show useful, current information but cannot guarantee every displayed detail will remain complete, accurate or available at booking time.

The binding price and terms are those shown by the travel provider when the booking is completed. Users should verify all material details before paying.

3. Deals and recommendations

A “good deal” label, ranking or recommendation is a decision-support tool based on information available to Ariella and the user’s preferences. It is not a guarantee of the lowest market price or the best option for every user.

4. Third-party booking

After leaving Ariella for a provider, the flight transaction is with that provider under its terms. The provider is responsible for confirmation, ticketing, flight payment, changes, cancellations, refunds and flight-related service.

5. Paid monitoring

Under the current model, each monitoring purchase is a one-time payment for 30 days at the selected frequency. There is no automatic renewal and no additional charge without a new user action and approval.

Ariella may send a reminder before the period ends and offer another 30-day period. Without renewal, monitoring ends with no further charge.

6. Alerts

Users who opt in may receive mobile or email alerts. Alerts are supplementary; current offer details must be verified with the travel provider before booking.

7. Affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate links. Ariella may receive compensation when a user follows such a link and/or completes a booking with a third party.

8. Accounts and acceptable use

Users are responsible for information supplied and account security. Security bypass, unauthorized scraping, impersonation and unlawful use are prohibited.

9. Availability

Ariella is under continuous development. Outages, errors and changes may occur. Subject to applicable law, uninterrupted availability or a result for every search is not guaranteed.

10. Updates and contact

These terms may be updated. The current version will be published on the website. Questions can be submitted through the Contact page.
